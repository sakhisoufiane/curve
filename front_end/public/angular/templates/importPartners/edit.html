Overview
Identifiers

Use the Work Identifiers sent in the CWR/Catalogue Shipment as the Main Identifiers of the Work on Curve. Only enable this option if this catalogue is your own content. If you are importing a 3rd party catalogue, we recommend you leave this option disabled as their Work Identifiers may overlap with your own. It is important these Main Identifiers are unique and consistent over time, as societies use these as references when importing your CWR deliveries.

Use the Composer's or Publisher's Interested Party # in the CWR/Catalogue Shipment as the Interested Party # of the Composers and Publishers on Curve. If you are importing a 3rd party catalogue we recommend you leave this option disabled as their Interested Party # may overlap with your own. It is important these Party #'s are unique and consistent over time, as some societies use these as reference when importing your CWR deliveries.
